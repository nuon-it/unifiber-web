# UNIFIBER RETAIL DIGITAL PLATFORM  
## Enterprise-Grade Connected Home & Entertainment Ecosystem

---

# Overview

Design a complete enterprise-grade digital platform for **Unifiber**, a premium FTTH (Fiber-to-the-Home) internet service provider in Indonesia transitioning from B2B infrastructure to full B2C retail operations.

Unifiber is collaborating with **Nuon (nuon.id)** to integrate digital entertainment services into the platform, including:

- Game top-up
- Digital vouchers
- Concert ticket purchases
- Football match ticketing
- Entertainment promotions

The platform must include:

- Mobile App (Primary Experience – iOS & Android)
- Desktop Web App (Responsive & Adaptive)
- Scalable Design System
- Enterprise-ready UI architecture
- Modular ecosystem integration layer

The experience must reflect:

- Network reliability
- Transparency
- Digital-first mindset
- Premium telecom positioning
- Entertainment-ready ecosystem expansion

---
